About us

A school built on opportunity, discipline, and excellence.

Hibiscus High School is a purpose-driven learning institution dedicated to helping every learner reach their full potential. Located in Rwamabondo, Kajara, Ntungamo District, we serve students from surrounding communities and beyond with a warm, disciplined, and academically focused environment.

Our school values are rooted in fairness, dignity, and a belief that every child deserves access to quality education. As a PEAS school, we support educational equity and inclusive growth in African schools, inspired by the mission of promoting equality in education.

Day & Boarding

Comfortable learning spaces for both day and residential students.

Our school offers a strong day programme and a boarding option that provides a structured, secure, and nurturing environment for students who need residential support. We strive to create a home away from home while maintaining academic focus.

  • Well-supervised boarding facilities
  • Safe and disciplined school environment
  • Focused academic routines and mentorship
  • Supportive community for students and families
